## Q1 — Is there any inbound route to a running subagent?

The owner asked for *"a user message input"* inside the subagent view.

**Do not conclude from an absent name.** An earlier answer here claimed "no inbound route exists" citing `subagent:changed` being a leaf channel — and that was **refuted during the grill**, correctly: proving one channel is outbound proves nothing about whether any inbound route exists. That is #90's and #116's mistake in both its directions (a route unreachable by name that was reachable by calling, and vice versa).

**Probe by CALLING**, on a warm handle, with a **bogus-subtype negative control** — without one, a `success` is indistinguishable from a dispatcher that swallows anything. #116's own harness is the template and its refusal text (`Unsupported control request subtype: …`) is the control you are looking for.

Also record: what `#104` established about the `Agent` tool being **async** on this CLI (parent turn and subagent settle independently), and whether anything in the SDK's typings or runtime exposes a per-subagent send. Typings alone settle nothing — `#115` is the precedent, where a declared wire type had no callable route.

## Q3 — What would "background a session" even mean here?

The owner said *"there should be a keybind that you can backgrund sessions like the / bg doesnt work"*.

**The premise is false and this is on the record.** `/bg` is one of three ways to **open the CLI's agent view** — *"empty prompt, `claude agents`, or `/bg`"* (`.context/flows.md`) — a whole-terminal takeover, not a command that backgrounds anything. It is also absent from the advertised 121. That is very likely why it "doesn't work".

Measure: is there any route by which this app could put its **currently open** session into the background so the CLI keeps working on it while the UI detaches? #91 shipped the background-session listing with *"Out of scope, explicitly: attach, peek, reply, dispatch"* and **no write route is recorded**. Report whether one exists, and at what cost — #90 measured the read side at ~893ms of CLI subprocess per look, so a write side's cost is a real finding, not a footnote.

## Rules this spike inherits

- **Probe by calling, never by grepping a bundle or reading a `.d.ts`.** A declared wire type is not a callable route (#115); a callable route is not an effective one (#117).
- **Every arm needs a negative control**, or its success is unscored.
- **Zero-turn where possible.** Control requests on a warm handle send no prompt and burn no turn (#105's economy). Say in the output which arms cost turns.
- **Import the app's real `cli-path.ts` and `backend-mode.ts`** so the binary and routing cannot drift from what the app spawns. Use `fileURLToPath`, never `URL.pathname` — this repo's path contains a space. `node --experimental-strip-types` is required on this Node (22.17).
- **Scrub the findings file.** Counts, shapes, vocabularies and timings — never raw paths, home directories or usernames.
- **An instrument that fails its own setup must report that as an unscored run**, never as the phenomenon. Three separate instances of that error are on this record.
- **A harness whose premise a later ticket fixes needs a drift alarm**, or its success becomes indistinguishable from its failure (#105 post-#112).
