## Do

Give the subagent transcript viewer the window's acrylic material, as the owner asked.

## Why this is allowed, and why it is not a one-line CSS change

`DESIGN.md`'s "Bans in force" reads *"no decorative extra glass layers inside the window (the OS acrylic is the one glass)"*, and #98 deliberately shipped the viewer at `var(--surface)` with **no** `backdrop-filter`, leaving the question *"whether the glass ban reaches a `var(--surface)` pane at all"* **unresolved** and on the "Do not decide these" list.

The owner has now named this surface directly. #98's own reasoning is what makes that decisive: it split the owner's instruction into what the owner **stated** (executed as given) and what the owner **did not state** (settled against the record) — and *"instruction did not state — size, motion, material, focus — was settled against"* the record. **Material sat in the second bucket only because the owner had not named it.** It is now named, which moves it into the first by #98's own division. This is not overturning a call the record argued; it is supplying the input the record explicitly lacked.

## The full cost — all four parts are required

1. **`subagent.css`** — add the material to the viewer pane.
2. **Retire `gui-98` criterion 5 explicitly.** That driver greps `subagent.css` for zero `backdrop-filter`, with the note *"the non-goal that keeps the unresolved glass-ban question harmless"*. Acrylic **reds it**. Do not weaken the driver into silence — **replace the negative criterion with a positive one** that asserts the material IS present. `.context/decisions/2026-08-04-an-unchanged-box-is-measured-in-run-not-across-the-edit.md` records that *"A previous audit round proposed conforming them and was refuted"*, and the countermeasure was exactly a positive pin, so a later tidy-up reds instead of passing quietly.
3. **Amend `DESIGN.md`'s "Bans in force" line** to record the exception and its scope. An undocumented deviation is the precise shape a conformance pass removes — #96's commit is literally `style: two off-scale values conform to DESIGN.md`.
4. **Write an ADR** in `.context/decisions/` recording that the owner named material, that this closes the #98 question **for this pane only**, and that the two anti-modal ADRs which cite the glass ban as rationale are **not** superseded.

## Scope, tightly

**This pane only.** The owner named the subagent viewer. Nothing authorises extending acrylic to the model menu, the command popover, the Appearance dock or any other `var(--surface)` surface — all of which share the viewer's current treatment. That generalisation is recorded as an open owner call in `.claude/vibe.md`; do not take it.

## Constraints

- **No GUI driver can observe a DWM backdrop** — `page.screenshot()` cannot show one, and `--disable-gpu` flattens acrylic (#117, #69). The pin is therefore on the **declaration**, read as text, not on rendered pixels. Do not write a driver that "verifies" the blur visually; it will lie.
- `backdrop-filter` has **no cost measurement and no DWM-interaction measurement** on record anywhere in this repo. If it visibly degrades scrolling in the viewer, say so in a ticket comment rather than shipping it silently.
- The viewer's 820px pane width is derived term-by-term (`760 + 48 + 2 + 10`) so `.chat-column` measures its documented 760 in **both** scroll states. Do not disturb it.
- The `.subagent-drawer*` class names are kept deliberately — seven files select on them. Do not rename.
- The neutrals are **not** re-tuned per backdrop (`DESIGN.md`, #69). If the pane reads too dark or too light over acrylic, that is a theme value or a defect — not a coupling to build.
